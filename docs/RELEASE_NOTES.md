- **Weekly meal plan from your rules.** Set required and preferred rules ("1–2 fish recipes", "a soup if possible", "one without meat") using Mealie tags, categories and foods, and choose which meals to plan each day. The rules come first, then the recipes with the most ingredients on sale. The week is planned locally or with AI, and either way it is checked against the rules.
- **Edit the week freely.** Change, remove, lock or swap any meal, before or after saving. Only the meals that changed are written to Mealie's meal planner.
- **Offers from Lidl, Kaufland and Billa.** Offers come from the shops' websites for free and, with AI, from their printed brochures. Each brochure is read once and kept until its offers end. Every offer keeps its own dates.
- **Promotions page.** All three shops' offers in one place, with filters by shop, category, date and discount, and sorting by discount, price, price per kg, name, expiry or shop.
- **The week's products, by shop.** The ingredients are added up and each is matched to the cheapest offer valid that week, or to no shop. Open a product to switch to a similar one from any shop. Nothing is added automatically: tick products and add them to HomeBasket Lists, each with its shop's zone and a note of the price and dates.
